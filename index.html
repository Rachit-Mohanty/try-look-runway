Return & Refund Policy

30-Day Window
Return any unworn apparel or footwear within 30 calendar days of delivery with original tags, labels, and unmarked shoebox intact.
Free Exchange
Complimentary size swaps across EU footwear and apparel sizes, subject to warehouse stock availability.
Refund Timeline
Quality inspection completes within 48 hours of doorstep pickup. Full refunds issue to your original UPI, card, or bank account within 5–7 business days.
Look Bundles
Individual pieces from a 10% curated Look Bundle can be returned or exchanged separately; refunds reflect the pro-rated bundle price paid.

SSL / HTTPS & PCI DSS Compliance

TLS 1.3 / SSL
All storefront browsing, catalog queries, and cart handoffs are enforced over HTTPS with HSTS and strict mixed-content blocking.
PCI DSS Level 1
Compliant with PCI DSS SAQ-A standards: card numbers, CVVs, and PINs never touch browser storage or application memory and are processed solely on the tokenized HTTPS checkout.
Privacy & Headers
Enforces X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, strict-origin-when-cross-origin referrer rules, and restricted device permissions.
